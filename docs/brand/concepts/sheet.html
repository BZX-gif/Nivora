NIVORA — logo concept exploration

Monochrome only · current mark unchanged · for evaluation

Concept A — Architectural N

Letterform

Two piers of unequal height joined by one sloped span. The letter becomes a roofline in profile; the rising diagonal carries the ascent.

Concept B — Abstract doorway

Sense of place

A solid field with an opening cut out of it. The threshold is the mark — no roof, walls or pin, so it reads as arriving somewhere rather than as a building.

Concept C — Geometric spatial symbol

Abstract geometry

Two planes seen in plan, offset so the negative space becomes a passage between them. The most abstract of the three.
